# Vertical video storyboard — 75 seconds

## Format

9:16, bilingual on-screen keywords, German voiceover with English subtitles.

| Time | Visual | Voiceover / on-screen copy |
|---|---|---|
| 0–5s | Close-up of dishwasher timer | “Eine bessere Stunde beginnt nicht mit dem ganzen Haushalt.” |
| 5–13s | Three appliance icons appear | “Beginne mit einem flexiblen Gerät.” |
| 13–23s | Phone screen represented by a neutral UI, no real customer data | “Prüfe, welche Informationen Tarif, App oder Gerät anbieten.” |
| 23–34s | Calendar/time selector; safety shield remains visible | “Wähle eine sichere Zeit, die zu deinem Alltag passt.” |
| 34–46s | Device scheduled; person leaves kitchen | “Teste die Routine eine Woche lang.” |
| 46–58s | Three reflection cards: convenience, clarity, keep/change | “War sie bequem? Waren die Informationen klar? Würdest du sie beibehalten?” |
| 58–68s | Myth card is crossed out: “Always cheaper at night” | “Es gibt keine Uhrzeit, die für alle Tarife und Haushalte gilt.” |
| 68–75s | Campaign title | “Eine flexible Anwendung. Eine bessere Stunde.” |

## Production notes

- Use an original neutral interface rather than reproducing an E.ON product screen.
- Do not show unattended unsafe appliance operation.
- Subtitle all spoken words.
- Keep the independent-case disclaimer in the caption and end card.
- Music must be original or properly licensed.
