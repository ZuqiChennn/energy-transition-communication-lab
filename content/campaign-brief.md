# Campaign brief — Shift the Hour

## Assignment

Create a four-week bilingual social campaign that helps German households understand energy flexibility without making universal price-saving claims.

## Audience

**Primary:** renters and homeowners aged 25–45 who use a washing machine, dishwasher or EV charger but do not consider themselves energy experts.

**Secondary:** sustainability-interested employees and partners who need a simple, accurate explanation they can share.

## Tension

People are told to “use energy smarter”, but the instruction is abstract. Convenience, tariff type and household routines matter more than another list of generic efficiency tips.

## Proposition

**One flexible appliance. One better hour.**

The audience starts with a single load and checks the information available from its tariff, app or device before changing a routine.

## Message ladder

1. **Awareness:** timing can matter, not only total consumption.
2. **Understanding:** flexible loads include dishwashers, washing machines, heat pumps and EV charging, when safe and convenient.
3. **Action:** check tariff/app/device information and schedule one load.
4. **Reflection:** review comfort and available consumption/cost information after one week.

## Channel roles

- **LinkedIn:** corporate-energy context, expert explainers and employee advocacy.
- **Instagram carousel:** behavior-led education and saveable checklists.
- **Reels/Shorts:** one-appliance demonstration in under 90 seconds.
- **Website/app landing page:** method, caveats and next-step information.

## Tone

Clear, useful and non-judgmental. Avoid guilt, techno-utopian language and unsupported savings promises.

## Safeguards

- Never suggest unattended operation where manufacturer or fire-safety guidance advises otherwise.
- State that price effects depend on tariff structure.
- Do not equate an illustrative system signal with the customer’s live local electricity mix.
- Do not imply that household behavior alone solves grid constraints.
- Route account, tariff and safety questions to the appropriate official service information.

## Independent-case disclaimer

Every public artifact must state that this is an independent portfolio case based solely on public information and is not endorsed by E.ON.
