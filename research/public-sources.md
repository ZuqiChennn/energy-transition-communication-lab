# Public sources and claim boundaries

Reviewed July 2026.

## E.ON Sustainability Factbook 2025

Source: <https://annualreport.eon.com/content/dam/eon-annualreport/documents/en/EON%20Sustainability%20Factbook%202025.pdf>

Used to understand E.ON’s public framing of growth, sustainability, digitalization, networks, infrastructure solutions and energy retail. The campaign does not reproduce branded language as if it were an approved E.ON campaign.

## E.ON Press & Newsroom

Source: <https://www.eon.com/en/ueber-uns/presse.html>

Used to observe the range of public topics, including networks, storage, electrification, mobility and affordability. No post-level engagement data were collected or inferred.

## Eurostat household electricity prices

Source: <https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/web/products-eurostat-news/w/ddn-20260505-1>

Eurostat reported an EU average of €28.96 per 100 kWh in the second half of 2025 and €38.69 in Germany. This contextual statistic can motivate public interest but is not used to promise campaign-induced savings.

## Eurostat dataset methodology

Source: <https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/cache/metadata/EN/nrg_pc_204_sims_me.htm>

Used to understand consumption bands and weighted price methodology. National averages must not be presented as an individual household’s tariff.

## Claim boundaries

- “Timing can matter” is used as a conditional system and tariff concept.
- “Better hour” means an hour supported by relevant available information and household constraints; it does not mean one universal clock time.
- No live carbon-intensity claim is made.
- No financial saving is quantified.
- No E.ON product functionality is asserted.
