# KPI and learning framework

No observed campaign results are claimed. The values displayed in the microsite are planning targets for an illustrative four-week pilot.

## Measurement model

| Role | Metric | Definition | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|
| Reach | Qualified reach | Unique viewers in the defined German household-energy audience | Is distribution reaching the intended audience? |
| Attention | 75% video completion | Viewers reaching 75% divided by video starts | Does the demonstration hold attention? |
| Utility | Save rate | Saves divided by impressions | Is the content useful enough to revisit? |
| Conversation | Meaningful comment rate | On-topic questions/experiences divided by impressions | Is the message prompting substantive discussion? |
| Action | Guide action rate | Landing-page checklist starts divided by unique visits | Does content move people to the next step? |
| Trust guardrail | Correction/complaint rate | Substantiated accuracy or overclaim complaints divided by impressions | Is clarity being preserved while scaling reach? |
| Safety guardrail | Unsafe-operation reports | Credible reports that content could encourage unsafe use | Stop or revise content immediately |

## Illustrative pilot targets

- 75% video completion: **≥ 32%**
- carousel save rate: **≥ 2.5%**
- guide action rate: **≥ 8% of unique landing-page visits**
- substantiated correction/complaint rate: **< 0.2%**
- unsafe-operation reports: **0**

These are portfolio assumptions, not E.ON benchmarks.

## Test plan

### Test A — proposition

- A: “One flexible appliance. One better hour.”
- B: “Four steps to smarter timing.”
- Primary metric: guide action rate
- Guardrail: correction/complaint rate

### Test B — caveat placement

- A: tariff caveat in the second carousel card
- B: tariff caveat in the final card and caption
- Primary metric: carousel completion
- Guardrail: tariff-related correction comments

### Interpretation

Do not declare a winner on click-through alone. Select the version that improves the primary action without weakening trust or safety guardrails.
